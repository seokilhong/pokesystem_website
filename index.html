Strategy

Where we find opportunity

Perpetual markets are spread across hundreds of contracts, and most of the flow in them comes from leveraged traders reacting to the same information at different speeds. For minutes to hours, individual contracts are pushed away from where their relationship to the rest of the market says they should be. We research these statistical relationships — in price, liquidity, positioning and funding — using full market data we collect ourselves.

How we build the portfolio

Many weak, mutually low-correlated signals are combined into one long–short portfolio across the most liquid contracts, so the result depends on the behaviour of the pool rather than on any single idea, and carries little exposure to the direction of the market. Each exchange runs its own independent portfolio, and a blend across exchanges runs a smoother path than any one of them.

How research reaches live trading

A candidate earns a place only if its edge survives realistic costs, adds something the portfolio does not already have, and can be executed at the intended size. Live results are compared with their simulations every day, and what live trading teaches feeds back into the next round of research.

Track record

Nearly three years of live, verifiable results.

The strategies have traded client and proprietary accounts continuously since late 2023 and have been profitable in every calendar year since launch — roughly +100% a year at 1× leverage, with an information ratio of around 2.5 on a conservative blended basis. Drawdowns are part of the record: the largest have been in the low twenties, and the one that began in July 2026 is still being recovered.

Results differ by exchange, by leverage and by when an allocation started, so no single figure describes every account. We share the full daily return series of each exchange as a workbook, and a read-only API key on a live account so the stream can be checked directly at the exchange.

Methodology

Performance is measured from actual account balance changes, net of trading fees and funding, before performance fees, non-compounded, at 1× leverage, per exchange and as a blended allocation across exchanges, with figures stated as of the end of the latest completed month.

Research

Built in live markets, improved by what they taught us.

The system went live on one exchange in late 2023 and was extended to others as independent systems rather than copies. Years of daily reconciliation between simulated and live results have shown where model and market diverge — execution costs that vary by exchange and by hour, contracts whose behaviour changes after listing, periods when strategies that had been consistently profitable lose together — and each finding has changed how the next round of research is specified. The drawdown of summer 2026 was of that last kind: it led to new data sources, low-correlation strategies on larger contracts, and a wider research pipeline assisted by LLM tooling.

Structure

The account is yours.

Clients open the exchange account in their own name and keep the login. The system connects through one trade-only API key with no withdrawal rights. Every fill, fee and funding payment appears in the client's own account history at the exchange, visible at any time, and the client can stop the software by deleting the key.
