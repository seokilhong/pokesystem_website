How we trade

Data

Full market data collected directly from each venue since 2023 — trades, premium index, funding, open interest and order-book depth — at one-minute resolution, maintained continuously.

Research

A large pool of raw signals constructed to be mutually low-correlated. Selection and weighting are re-run weekly with statistical methods. Backtests are strictly point-in-time and include trading costs, latency and funding, so simulated and live results are compared like for like.

Portfolio

Market-neutral across BTC, ETH and 30–40 of the most liquid perpetuals. Positions are recalculated every minute with 300–400% daily turnover; net exposure stays within ±20% and has averaged about 5% in 2026. Each venue runs its own independent system.

Risk

Sizing, not stop-losses. No single coin exceeds 10% of the book, book size scales with the account balance, and exposure contracts through drawdowns and re-scales with recovery. Hard stops are avoided by design because they repeatedly forfeit the rebound that follows dislocations.

Operations

Fully automated, 24/7. Automated playbooks are the first responder to any incident — halting, reconciling positions against the exchange, reconnecting feeds or restarting a strategy — with every action logged and alerted. Instance health is monitored continuously.

How it has evolved

Binance live in November 2023; OKX added in 2024; Bybit in 2025. In 2026, after the summer drawdown, a premium data source and low-correlation large-cap strategies were added and research was widened with LLM-assisted tooling; realised volatility roughly halved in the second half of the year. Current work: capacity toward US$30–50M, venue-specific execution research, and client-facing reporting.

Your account

The account is yours. We hold the key, not the funds.

Clients open the exchange account in their own name and keep the login. The system connects through one trade-only API key: no withdrawal rights, restricted to our servers. Every fill, fee and funding payment appears in the client's own account history at the exchange, visible at any time with the client's login or a read-only key given to an auditor. Our record of every position and balance is reconciled against the venue daily, and anything we cannot explain reaches the client before anything else. Deleting the key stops the software that second.
