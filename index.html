Quantitative trading systems for digital asset markets.

Pokesystem builds, tests and maintains them — and deploys them on clients' own exchange accounts. We never hold client funds.

What we build

One pipeline, from market data to live orders.

Each system covers the whole chain — data collection, research, portfolio construction, execution and monitoring. The software runs on infrastructure we operate and connects to a client's exchange account through trade-only API keys.

Clients keep custody of their funds and control of their API permissions at all times. We never request withdrawal rights.

How it is built

Data

Full market data collected directly from each venue — trades, premium index, funding, open interest and order-book depth — stored at one-minute resolution and maintained continuously.

Research

A large pool of raw signals built on that data, constructed to be mutually low-correlated. Selection and weighting are re-run periodically with statistical methods. Backtests are strictly point-in-time and include trading costs, latency and funding, so simulated and live behaviour are compared like for like.

Portfolio and execution

Market-neutral statistical arbitrage across the most liquid perpetual contracts. Positions are recalculated every minute and placed through an execution layer tuned to each venue. Every venue runs its own independent system.

Deployment and controls

Each account is configured independently — venue, instrument set, sizing and risk limits — and monitored around the clock, with automated alerts on data, connectivity and position anomalies.
